# Echo — Roadmap

What is done and what comes next, so every session starts from the same plan.
Details of each system live in `CLAUDE.md`.

## Done
- Level room grids, Echo Shards, Sentinels (guardians), lockdown rooms, collapse escape
- Health in segments, partner mending, downed / revive, distress pulse
- Controllers (each player's own gamepad, twin-stick aiming)
- Audio pass (procedural sounds, adaptive music, ambience)
- 3/4 world view, landmarks, harmless life
- Skills (offers, choice screen, actives / passives) and the pause menu
- The Angler (stage 1 boss) on the reusable boss framework
- UI style PART 1 + 2a: shared Theme, new menu flow, Journey screen
- Journey islands in the "vessel of the Ancient Light" art direction (Ocean: Sea Globe)

## Now
- **UI style PART 2b**: the silhouette + glow Theme (GlowButton, PulseReveal,
  ScreenFader, drawn prompts) on every remaining screen: options panel, story text
  screens, pause menu, stage cleared summary, defeat screen, confirm dialogs, and the
  in-game HUD (pressure bars, health segments, shard counter, skill icons + cooldowns,
  boss title + HP bar; readable and minimal, debug lines behind F1). A short fade
  between levels that also hides the level build time (~0.5-1.2 s). Arabic RTL
  checked on every screen.

## Next
1. **Step 6 — story text**: the intro and the Ocean ending, Arabic + English.
2. **Step 7 — unique bosses** for stages 2-3 (replacing The Hollow Brute
   placeholder) and Ocean polish.

## Later
- The other four worlds (Caves, Forest, Desert, Snow): rooms, levels, creatures,
  pressure, music (the Journey already has their islands and placeholder themes).
